# ⚡ Enable GitHub Pages in 30 Seconds

Your website files are ready and pushed to GitHub. Now just enable Pages:

## Step 1: Go to Settings
Open this link in your browser:
```
https://github.com/andreolf/vitalik-hunt-web/settings/pages
```

## Step 2: Configure Source
Under **"Build and deployment"**:
- **Source:** Select `Deploy from a branch`
- **Branch:** Select `main`
- **Folder:** Select `/ (root)`

## Step 3: Click Save

That's it! GitHub will deploy your site within 1-2 minutes.

---

## 🌐 Your Site Will Be Live At:
```
https://andreolf.github.io/vitalik-hunt-web
```

---

## ✅ Verify Deployment
1. Go to **Settings → Pages**
2. Look for a green checkmark next to your deployment
3. Click the URL to view your live site
